What You Can Do with TabLinker

Mac-first session power, plus cross-device organization.

Import from 13 Browsers

Pull in every open tab from Safari, Chrome, Arc, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera, and more — with a folder created automatically for each browser window.

Save From Anywhere

Grab the current tab with a keyboard shortcut on Mac, or save from any app on iPhone and iPad using the Share Sheet or the Safari extension.

Restore a Session, or One Window

Reopen an entire research session in one click, or pick out just the window you need. Links open in whichever browser you prefer.

Browse by Window

A dedicated By Window view groups tabs by the browser window they came from, so a 150-tab import stays easy to read.

Select Many at Once

Multi-select tabs to move or delete them in one go. On iPhone and iPad, just drag down the list to select a whole run of them.

Folders, Tags, Notes, and Pins

Organize with custom folder icons and colors, add tags and notes, and pin the folders you use most to the top of the list.

Recently Deleted

Deleted something you needed? Anything you remove is recoverable for 30 days, the same way Reminders and Photos work.

Search Everything

Find any saved tab by title, URL, note, or tag — instantly, and without leaving the app. Your tabs show up in Spotlight too.

Export However You Need

JSON, HTML, Markdown, Plain Text, and Rich Text — or build your own layout with custom templates. Import from JSON, HTML, or a pasted list of URLs.

Apple Shortcuts

Automate TabLinker with Shortcuts actions to save a URL, create a folder, list what is inside one, or open everything in it.

Live in the Menu Bar

Keep TabLinker in the menu bar and out of your Dock, so saving a session never means switching away from what you are doing.

Private iCloud Sync

Everything syncs across iPhone, iPad, and Mac through your own iCloud account. No TabLinker account, no ads, no tracking.
